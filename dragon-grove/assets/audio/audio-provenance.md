## Additional original sound design

Correct-answer chimes, the gentle retry cue, the growth and victory note sequences, thunder, woodland shimmer and Astral tones are generated by `dragon-grove/audio.js` using oscillators and filtered noise. They are original game effects, not a claim of a recorded orchestra or a recorded real dragon. Fire and wings use the recordings credited above.

Music and sound effects have independent switches. Audio begins only after an eligible user interaction. Question scenes lower the music level; reduced-effects mode lowers both buses. Hiding the document pauses music, stops current effects and suspends the audio context. The graph caps the master gain at 0.8, music bus at 0.2 and effects bus at 0.4, includes a compressor, filters effect sub-bass at 65 Hz, and limits active voices to 24.
