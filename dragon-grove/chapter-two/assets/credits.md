# The Skybound Trials: artwork and sound

The canyon, forest, coast and six organic challenge sprites were generated for Bright Quest using OpenAI image generation on 11 October 2026. No purchased Fal assets were used. The artwork was exported as WebP at quality 88; the sprite atlas retains its transparent background.

The dragon remains Regina Cachoa's **European Dragon**, with its textured sculpt, skeleton and authored animation. Model, music and sound recordings retain the licences and attributions in [the original credits](../../assets/credits.md). Music and sound settings carry across from Dragon Grove.

## Artwork briefs

- **Canyon flight:** realistic aerial woodland valley; ancient cliffs on both sides, winding river, distant mountains, warm golden light and mist. Keep the central 60% open for the animated dragon. No characters, text, interface or geometric props.
- **Forest sanctuary:** realistic ancient woodland; twisted mossy trunks on both sides, a broad clearing, spring water, ferns and vines, warm light and subtle luminous seed pods. Keep the centre open for play.
- **Storm coast:** realistic rugged basalt and limestone coast, emerald grass, dark teal ocean, islands and distant mountains. Moody clouds with warm breaks and distant lightning at the far right. Majestic and suitable for children, with an open flight corridor.
- **Organic sprite atlas:** six complete objects in a 3 by 2 transparent atlas, each with padding and consistent warm light from the upper left: mossy rock, thorn bramble, golden flying bird, luminous seed pod, violet smoke wisp and woven bird nest. Detailed natural textures; no grid, labels, floor or interface.

Landscapes are 1672 by 941 pixels. The transparent atlas is 1536 by 1024 pixels, with six 512-pixel cells. The renderer layers these assets around the animated 3D dragon and its mouth-attached power effects.

File fingerprints and dimensions are recorded in [provenance.json](provenance.json). The exact final prompts, output filenames and transparency settings are saved in [prompts.json](prompts.json). Generation used the built-in image tool.
